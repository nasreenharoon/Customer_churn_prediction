Model Performance Metrics

Accuracy (77%)

The percentage of total correct predictions. While baseline models without class-balancing report ~80% accuracy, they are highly biased. Our model trades 3% accuracy for a balanced decision boundary.

Precision (56% Churn)

The fraction of predicted churners who actually churned. Higher precision reduces wasted promotional campaign spend by targeting only the correct users.

Recall (70% Churn)

The fraction of actual churners identified by the model. **This is our primary KPI.** Maximizing recall ensures that we capture 70% of customers planning to leave.

F1-Score (62% Churn)

The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall, proving a balanced predictive quality over both false positives and false negatives.
